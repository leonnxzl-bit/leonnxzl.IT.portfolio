JuanVote (Capstone Project)

Project Manager · 2025 · Unity

As the project manager of JuanVote, I defined the project’s needs and guided a diverse team of developers, artists, and content creators toward achieving them. I assigned tasks, streamlined communication, and ensured everyone stayed aligned with our objectives. I set the design direction, planned sprints, evaluated team outputs, and made sure our gameplay and UI aligned with t he vision for youth voter education. Leading the team strengthened my ability to set priorities, coordinate collaboration, and solve problems creatively.

Our team also earned 2nd Runner-Up in the Android Application – Game Development category at UMak 2025 last November 21, 2025, recognizing our creativity, teamwork, and technical execution.

Whack-A-Potion

Game Application · 2024 · Unity

For the Whack-A-Potion game, we worked together to design and build a user interface that supports fast-paced and enjoyable gameplay. We created the main menu, in-game HUD, score and timer displays, and feedback effects that help guide the player during each round. As a team, we organized UI elements so the game feels clear, responsive, and easy to understand even during quick actions. We also developed the visual theme—colors, icons, and magical UI cues—to match the potion-themed style of the game. Overall, our collaborative UI work made the game more engaging, intuitive, and visually consistent.
