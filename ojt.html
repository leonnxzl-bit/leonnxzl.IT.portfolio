ON THE JOB TRAINING - IT&DS of Our lady of Fatima University (OLFU Valenzuela Campus)

As an IT Technical Support Intern at OLFU, I assisted in troubleshooting hardware, software, and network issues while supporting daily IT operations to ensure smooth and reliable campus technology.

HARDWARE

Hardware Maintenance & Troubleshooting

During my OJT, I worked with computer hardware by assembling, cleaning, and repairing desktops and laptops. I replaced components such as RAM, storage drives, power supplies, and peripherals to improve performance. I also performed diagnostics to identify issues, helped maintain equipment, and ensured every unit operated safely and efficiently. This experience improved my skills in troubleshooting and understanding how each part contributes to the overall system.

NETWORKING

Network Setup & Connectivity

For networking tasks, I assisted in setting up small LAN connections, configuring routers, and managing network cables. I helped check connectivity, assign IP addresses, and test network stability between devices. I became familiar with how network components work together to provide internet and shared resources. This experience improved my understanding of network structure, troubleshooting, and maintaining reliable communication between systems.

Supporting Images

OMADA

Omada Wireless Management

During my OJT, I worked with the TP-Link Omada network management system in OLFU-ITDS to monitor and manage network devices. I assisted in configuring access points, checking signal coverage, and ensuring stable Wi-Fi connectivity in different areas. I learned how to use the Omada Controller to view connected clients, adjust settings, update firmware, and troubleshoot connection issues. This helped me understand how centralized networking works and how multiple devices can be controlled from one dashboard. My experience with Omada improved my skills in wireless networking, monitoring, and maintaining reliable network performance for users.

TECHNICAL SUPPORT

User Support & Software Issues

As part of technical support, I assisted users with software installation, configuration, and basic troubleshooting. I helped resolve common problems such as slow performance, connection errors, and application issues. I learned how to communicate effectively with users, document problems, and provide solutions in a clear and polite way. This experience strengthened my ability to handle real-world IT concerns and support people in a professional environment.
